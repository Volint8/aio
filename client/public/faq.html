What is Apraizal?

Apraizal is a Volint product for performance management. It combines task tracking, OKR tracking, team dashboards, work approvals, and AI-assisted appraisal reports.

Who should use Apraizal?

Apraizal is useful for HR admins, founders, operations teams, managers, and team leads that need a clearer record of employee and team performance.

What makes Apraizal different?

Apraizal connects everyday execution to appraisal reporting. Instead of writing reviews only from memory, managers can use tasks, OKRs, deadlines, submissions, and approved contributions.

Can Apraizal track KPIs?

Yes. Apraizal can support KPI tracking, employee KPI reviews, team KPI tracking, and KPI-based appraisal reporting. Some organizations call these OKRs, while others call them KPIs or performance indicators.

Can Apraizal support promotion or salary reviews?

Yes. Apraizal can generate decision-support reports for promotion, salary review, performance review, layoffs, skills gap analysis, and other appraisal purposes. Human manager review is still required.
